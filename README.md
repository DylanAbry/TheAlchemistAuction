# The Alchemist Auction

A fantasy strategy RPG where players bid, battle, steal, and survive against the deceptive alchemist mastermind, Dr. deFraud, and his partner Fleece deFraud.

Built in Unity with C#, Alchemist Auction combines auction-based resource management, probability-driven AI behavior, potion crafting systems, mini-games, and multi-phase boss encounters into a single gameplay experience.

Features
Strategic auction gameplay with dynamic bidding systems
AI opponents that make probability-based decisions
Potion crafting and inventory management systems
Multi-phase RPG-style boss battles
Persistent progression and unlock systems
Interactive hub world ("Completionist Cave")
Bonus mini-games and challenge modes
Animated UI systems and in-world interactions
Original fantasy worldbuilding and characters
Gameplay Overview

Players compete in high-stakes magical auctions where every decision matters. Buy powerful potions, sabotage opponents, manage resources, and survive increasingly dangerous encounters.

The game features:

Auction rounds with adaptive AI bidding
Trade-in systems and potion collection mechanics
"Pilfer Pass" stealing mechanics
Risk/reward inventory decisions
Bonus challenge games for additional rewards
A large-scale boss fight against Dr. deFraud and Fleece deFraud
